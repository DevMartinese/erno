erno

A tiny engine for twisty puzzles rendered to SVG. N×N cubes, Skewb, Pyraminx, Megaminx, Mirror, Void, Tetris, cuboids, shape mods and turners: moves, scrambles, layer animation and per-sticker styling. No WebGL, no canvas, just <svg>. Sibling of heerich.

Algorithm

Moves

Shift-click for the inverse (R → R′).

Build

0.33

Depth 0 cuts through the middle; 0.33 is a Dino, 0.5 a Helicopter. Most values are puzzles nobody makes.
